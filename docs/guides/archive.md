# Archive and deletion

## Archiving {#archive}

"Delete" in the app means **archive**. Archived documents and folders are hidden from everyone except the main administrator, their public links stop working immediately and reminders stop. Archives are kept indefinitely.

By default only the main administrator can archive. The **Archive** page lets the main administrator restore documents or delete them permanently.

## Permanent deletion {#purge}

Permanent deletion (type the title to confirm) removes all versions, previews, thumbnails and search data of that document, leaving only an audit entry. It cannot be undone in the app.

Copies in earlier backups, exports, downloads and offline devices are independent and follow their own retention; permanent deletion does not erase them.
